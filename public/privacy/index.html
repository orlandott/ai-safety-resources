Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

The short version

AI Safety Resources — both the website and the mobile app — is a catalog you browse. We don't require accounts, we don't collect personal data, and we don't use ads, analytics SDKs, or trackers in the mobile app.

Mobile app

The AI Safety Resources mobile app stores your reading list and progress (saved and finished resources) only on your device. This data never leaves your phone, is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and is deleted when you delete the app. The app requests no permissions and contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code. Resource cover images are loaded from the public websites that host them (for example Wikipedia); those requests are subject to the host's own policies.

Website

Browsing the website requires no account. Reading-list progress is stored in your browser's local storage. If you choose to create an optional sync account, we store the email address you provide and your reading-list data solely to provide cross-device sync; we never sell or share it. If you submit a suggestion or contact message, the details you enter are emailed to the site maintainer to act on your message and are not used for anything else.

Contact

Questions about this policy or requests to delete data you've submitted: use the Contact button on the homepage.
