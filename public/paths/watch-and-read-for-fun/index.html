Watch & read for fun

Pure story. The films, series, and novels that dramatize machine minds, agency, and the alignment problem—an enjoyable way in that happens to teach the core ideas.

  1. 1
    Ex MachinaFilms Beginner~2 hr watch

    A near-perfect chamber piece on the alignment test.

  2. 2
    HerFilms Beginner~2 hr watch

    The gentlest AI film ever made—and quietly one of the most unsettling.

  3. 3
    The MatrixFilms Beginner~2.5 hr watch

    The machine-takeover blockbuster that rewired pop culture.

  4. 4
    2001: A Space OdysseyFilms Beginner~2.5 hr watch

    HAL 9000: cinema's definitive misaligned AI, still unmatched.

  5. 5
    Blade RunnerFilms Beginner~2 hr watch

    The noir classic asking what separates made minds from born ones.

  6. 6
    WALL-EFilms Beginner~1.5 hr watch

    A robot love story that doubles as a fable about delegating too much to machines.

  7. 7
    AlphaGoDocumentaries Beginner~1.5 hr watch

    The real-world match that felt like science fiction.

  8. 8
    HumansTV Shows Beginner~45 min per episode

    A grounded near-future drama about personhood, control, and the minds we build.

  9. 9
    WestworldTV Shows Beginner~60 min per episode

    A lush puzzle-box about consciousness and what we owe the minds we make.

  10. 10
    Black MirrorTV Shows Beginner~60 min per episode

    Standalone near-future nightmares—start with the AI episodes and keep going.

  11. 11
    Person of InterestTV Shows Beginner~45 min per episode

    A network procedural that slow-burns into the best superintelligence story on TV.

  12. 12
    DevsTV Shows Beginner~50 min per episode

    A hypnotic techno-thriller about determinism and the hubris of Silicon Valley.

  13. 13
    PantheonTV Shows Beginner~45 min per episode

    An animated saga of uploaded minds racing toward the singularity.

  14. 14
    I, RobotFiction Books Beginner~6.5 hr read

    Asimov's robot stories are the original alignment case studies.

  15. 15
    Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep?Fiction Books Beginner~7.5 hr read

    The Philip K. Dick novel behind Blade Runner—stranger and sadder than the film.

  16. 16
    NeuromancerFiction Books Intermediate~8 hr read

    The book that invented cyberspace, with an AI pulling the strings.

  17. 17
    All Systems RedFiction Books Beginner~4.5 hr read

    Murderbot: a self-hacked security android who'd rather watch soap operas. Pure fun.

  18. 18
    Klara and the SunFiction Books Beginner~9 hr read

    A Nobel laureate's tender story told through the eyes of an artificial friend.
